A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired,
written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb).

## The monologue

> The eclipse, then. Third time this century I have climbed these stairs,
> dagger in hand, waiting for the moon to — yes. The moon. The ravens bring
> the omens before I ask, and half of them are wrong. Your king sent a bard
> to spy on me. He sleeps in the east tower, snoring. I have not decided:
> toad, or let him finish the verse. The rite needs three drops of royal
> blood and one signature. Bring the ink. Bring the quiet, too. If the stars
> could only... never mind. Kneel, or don't. The old throne is cold either
> way.

Exactly 100 words, 515 characters. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the
pauses and the small deflections — the speaker never says he is weary. He
is a specific villain with a history (the third eclipse this century) and
a scheme (the rite: three drops of royal blood and one signature).

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`, after checking it
against the competition gate (UTF-8 text, 1–600 characters, not only
whitespace). Run it with Node 22 or later:

```sh
node main.ts
```
